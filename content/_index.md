I'm a postdoc in the physics department at UC Berkeley, where I work on [Hamiltonian Monte Carlo methods for sampling]((https://blackjax-devs.github.io/sampling-book/algorithms/mclmc.html)) with applications in quantum physics and statistics. Before that I worked on Bayesian models of communication, using [probabilistic programming]() to try to understand how people reason about each other.

I'm currently interested in putting these strands (physics and cognitive science) together, and building dynamical systems models of multi-agent coordination. Here's a description of [the sort of approach I'm interested in](/blog/the-unfolding-of-language/).
